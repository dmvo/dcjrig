The 4824-3000-CP is an example of a low DIP24 socket.
Its nominal height is 5.08 mm, with a 0.127 mm general tolerance.
Add the 74F821 body height of up to 5.08 mm: the total is 10.287 mm.
This is an example for the height calculation. Measure the sockets you use.
